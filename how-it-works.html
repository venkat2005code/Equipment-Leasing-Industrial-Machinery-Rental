Simple Process

Renting Made Simple.
Delivery Made Fast.

From browsing to on-site delivery in as little as 48 hours. Our 4-step process is designed to eliminate friction and get the right machine to your site without delays.

01

Select Your Equipment

Browse our catalog of 850+ certified machines. Use filters to narrow by equipment type, weight class, lifting capacity, power output, or site-specific requirements. Each listing shows real-time availability, full specification sheets, and indicative daily rates.

  • Filter by 18 equipment categories
  • View full spec sheets and certification documents
  • Check real-time availability calendar
  • Compare multiple machines side by side
  • Save equipment to your project shortlist

02

Book Your Project Dates

Select your project start date and expected return date. Our booking calendar automatically shows availability for your chosen machine and flags any conflicts. Specify your delivery site — address, GPS coordinates, or site map upload — and any special access requirements.

  • Interactive calendar with availability view
  • Precise delivery location with map pin
  • Note special access restrictions (gates, height limits)
  • Flexible date extension during active rental

03

Confirm & Pay Securely

Review your complete quote — itemized by rental rate, insurance, mobilization logistics, and any requested extras. Upload required site documents (safety plan, site access form). Pay via secure bank transfer, credit card, or approved purchase order for corporate accounts.

  • Itemized quote — no surprise charges ever
  • Secure payment gateway (PCI-DSS compliant)
  • Corporate PO accepted for approved accounts
  • Instant confirmation email with booking reference
  • Digital rental agreement accessible any time

Your machine arrives cleaned, fueled, and fully inspected. Our field technician commissions it at your site, conducts a safety walkthrough with your team, and provides operator documentation. During your rental, track maintenance status and log service requests through your dashboard.

FAQs

Common Questions

Do rates include fuel?

No. Fuel arrangements are separate and flexible — we can supply fuel delivery or you can manage on-site fueling. Discussed during quote phase.

What if equipment breaks down?

Call our 24/7 support line. We guarantee a field technician on-site within 4 hours. For critical failures, we provide a replacement machine within 24 hours — at no extra cost.

Can I extend a rental mid-project?

Yes. Log in to your dashboard, go to your active rental, and select "Extend Dates." If the machine is available, extension is confirmed immediately with updated invoice.

Is insurance included?

All rentals include third-party liability and equipment damage insurance up to the declared value. Comprehensive on-site risk insurance is optional and quoted separately.
