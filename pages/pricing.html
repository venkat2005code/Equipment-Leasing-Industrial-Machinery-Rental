What affects pricing

Eight factors that shape your quotation

Understanding these up front helps us produce an accurate quote faster.

  • Volume

    Higher and steadier volumes support lower unit prices and dedicated capacity.

  • Service hours

    Business-hours coverage costs less than extended or 24/7 operation with night-shift premiums.

  • Support channels

    Voice requires more tooling and supervision than email or chat; multichannel blends fall in between.

  • Process complexity

    Specialised knowledge, multi-system work and regulated steps need deeper training and controls.

  • SLA commitments

    Tighter response, accuracy and resolution targets need additional staffing and quality effort.

  • Team size

    Larger teams share supervision and support costs, lowering the cost per seat.

  • Technology requirements

    Client-mandated platforms, integrations and security tooling affect set-up and running costs.

  • Languages and location

    Rare language skills and delivery-centre choice change the labour cost base.
