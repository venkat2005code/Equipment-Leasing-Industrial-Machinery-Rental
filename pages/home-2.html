Transformation roadmap

From baseline to steady state in about fourteen weeks

A structured path that protects customers and colleagues during change.

  1. Weeks 0–2

    Discover

    Understand the operation before changing anything.

    • Process and volume baseline
    • Risk and compliance review
    • Success measures agreed
  2. Weeks 2–4

    Design

    Turn the baseline into a service model.

    • Team structure and staffing plan
    • Workflows, tooling and controls
    • Reporting pack and governance calendar
  3. Weeks 4–10

    Transition

    Move work across without disrupting customers.

    • Recruitment and structured training
    • Nesting and parallel run
    • Go-live readiness sign-off
  4. Weeks 10–14

    Stabilise

    Reach steady-state performance.

    • Hypercare with daily reviews
    • Quality calibration
    • First SLA review with your leaders
  5. Ongoing

    Optimise

    Compound the gains every quarter.

    • Root-cause programmes
    • Automation and tooling opportunities
    • Business continuity rehearsals

Demo case study · Logistics

From a nine-day matching backlog to next-day invoicing

The challenge

A freight forwarder could not invoice until delivery documents were matched to shipments. Paper and photos arrived in every format, and matching took nine days.

Our approach

We standardised key fields by lane, matched automatically where possible and applied clear resolution rules to the rest, with daily reporting to operations.

The outcome

Matching now happens in under a day, invoicing accelerated by eleven days, and delivery-related disputes fell by 60%.

Security & business continuity

Resilient by design, audited by default

Distributed delivery only helps if it is secure and rehearsed. Our controls are documented, tested and shared with clients.

  • Information-security framework aligned to ISO 27001 (demo)
  • SOC 2-style control reporting (demo)
  • Annual continuity exercises

Role-based access, secured production areas, endpoint controls, encrypted transfer and full activity logging protect client data at every site.

Work can move between delivery centres or shift to remote operation. Recovery times are tested twice a year and reported to clients.

Independent internal audits, client audit rights and documented procedures keep programmes ready for regulatory review.
