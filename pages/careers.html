Why work with us

A place to grow, not just a place to work

Service operations are hard, human work. We build the environment that makes people want to stay.

Real career paths

Most of our team leaders and managers started as associates. Promotion decisions are based on published criteria, not tenure or politics.

Learning that sticks

Paid onboarding, nesting with experienced colleagues, certification support and a personal learning budget.

Work that matters

People who care

Team leaders who coach, peers who share knowledge and a culture where speaking up is expected.

Company culture

Coaching over command

Our floors are busy, not frantic. Team leaders spend most of their time coaching and removing obstacles, not policing the queue.

Learning, development & career growth

Structured ways to build your skills and your future

Skills you can build

  • Product and process training on day one
  • Communication and de-escalation workshops
  • Data, reporting and analytics courses
  • Lean and Six Sigma certification pathways

How learning works

New hires complete a paid classroom programme followed by nesting: supervised live work with a buddy. Ongoing learning is scheduled inside working hours, and every employee has an annual learning budget.

A typical progression

  1. 1Associate
  2. 2Senior Associate
  3. 3Team Leader
  4. 4Operations Manager
  5. 5Delivery Director

Promotion criteria are published for every level, and internal candidates are always considered first for open roles.

Recognition programmes

  • Monthly quality and customer-satisfaction awards
  • Peer-nominated “Difference Maker” recognition
  • Annual leadership excellence programme

Feedback that goes both ways

Quarterly engagement surveys and skip-level sessions make sure leaders hear directly from the people doing the work — and act on it.

Recruitment process

Five steps, clear communication

You will always know where you are in the process and what happens next.

  1. 01

    Apply

    Submit your profile online. We review every application and reply within five working days.

  2. 02

    Screening call

    A recruiter discusses your experience, expectations and language skills.

  3. 03

    Assessment

    Role-based exercise — a voice simulation, typing and accuracy test, or a case exercise.

  4. 04

    Interview

    Meet the hiring manager and a future colleague to talk through how you work.

  5. 05

    Offer and onboarding

    Background checks where permitted, then a structured, paid onboarding programme.
