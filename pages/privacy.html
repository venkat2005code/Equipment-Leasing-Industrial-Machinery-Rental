1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Alturis Global Services (“Alturis”, “we”) collects, uses and protects personal information when you visit this website, contact us or apply for a role.

Replace “Alturis” and the contact details in this document with your own organisation’s information.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide: name, business email, phone number, company, role, enquiry details, application materials and any other information you submit through our forms.

Information collected automatically: basic technical data such as browser type, pages visited and approximate location, if analytics is enabled by the site owner.

3. How we use information

We use personal information to respond to enquiries, prepare proposals, process job applications, operate and secure the website, and comply with legal obligations.

Marketing emails are sent only where you have opted in, and every message includes an unsubscribe link.

4. Legal bases

Where data protection laws such as the GDPR apply, we rely on consent, performance of a contract or steps before entering into one, legitimate interests and legal obligation, depending on the activity.

5. Sharing and processors

We share information only with service providers who process it on our behalf under written agreements — for example hosting, form-handling and email providers — and where required by law.

6. International transfers

Our delivery network spans several countries. Where personal data is transferred internationally, we use appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

7. Retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purpose collected. Enquiry data is typically retained for 24 months and unsuccessful job applications for 12 months, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

8. Security

We apply technical and organisational measures including access controls, encryption in transit, staff training and incident response procedures. No method of transmission is completely secure.

9. Your rights

Depending on your location you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, to object to processing, and to data portability. Contact us to exercise these rights.

10. Cookies and local storage

This template stores your theme (light or dark) and text-direction preference in your browser’s local storage so the site remembers your choice. It does not set tracking cookies. If you add analytics or embed third-party services, update this section accordingly.

11. Children

Our website and services are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of the page shows when it was last revised.

13. Contact us

For privacy questions, email partnerships@alturis-bpo.example or write to Alturis Global Services, 1200 Meridian Business Park, Suite 400, Austin, TX 78701, USA.
