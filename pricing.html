Optional Add-ons

Services You Can Add to Any Rental

Pay only for what your project actually needs. Add-ons are priced transparently and included in your itemized quote.

Certified Machine Operator

Trained, OSHA-certified operator supplied with the machine. Ideal when your own operator is unavailable or for specialist machinery requiring licensed personnel.

$180 – $280 per day

On-Site Fuel Delivery

Scheduled fuel replenishment delivered directly to your site by tanker. Eliminates off-site procurement logistics. Minimum 200-litre delivery.

+$0.18/L surcharge on pump price

Comprehensive On-Site Insurance

All rentals include standard third-party and equipment damage cover. Upgrade to full on-site risk insurance covering third-party property and personnel.

1.8% – 2.5% of declared equipment value/month

Dedicated Field Technician

A resident maintenance technician assigned exclusively to your site for the duration. Included in long-term fleet contracts; available as an add-on for 1-month+ rentals.

$320 – $450 per day (1-month min.)

Specialist Attachments

Rock breakers, hydraulic thumbs, augers, compaction wheels, grading blades and other machine-specific attachments hired alongside the base machine.

$40 – $320 per day (attachment-dependent)

Extended Delivery Radius

Standard delivery included within 50km of nearest depot. Remote site surcharge applies for 50km–200km. Beyond 200km quoted separately with logistics cost breakdown.

$2.20/km beyond 50km from depot

Running a Multi-Machine Site?
Ask About a Fleet Contract.

For projects requiring 5+ machines running continuously over 6+ months, we structure a managed fleet contract — not a rental. You get dedicated machines, a resident technician, guaranteed availability, and a single monthly invoice for the entire site fleet.

  • Resident fleet technician assigned to your site full-time
  • Single consolidated monthly invoice for entire fleet
  • Swap any underperforming machine within 48 hours, no penalty
